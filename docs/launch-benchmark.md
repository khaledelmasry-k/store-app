## Positioning decision

**Category:** Commerce Operating System for Arabic-first merchants.

**Primary promise:** **كل تجارتك من مكان واحد** — Matjari connects the merchant’s actual operating loop instead of presenting a collection of disconnected tools.

**Core flow:** `Store → Order → Shipping → Customer → CRM → Reports`

**Supporting proof:**

- A real storefront, cart and checkout feed the merchant order workspace.
- Products, variants, stock, customers and order history share the same tenant data.
- Shipping configuration and Wasla readiness are visible without promising automatic production shipment creation.
- CRM notes, follow-ups and customer history sit beside operational data.
- Sales Links and landing pages connect acquisition to recorded orders.
- Reports use captured sales, cost and order data; private profitability data stays merchant-only.

## Launch packaging

- Free is one onboarding window of 30 days, not a free-forever plan.
- Starter: 499 EGP/month.
- Growth: 799 EGP/month and the only “Most Popular / الأكثر طلبًا” plan.
- Pro: 1,299 EGP/month.
- Lifetime Access is a separate one-time product and approval flow.
- Business remains an archived legacy plan for historical subscriptions and is not offered to new merchants.

## Claim guardrail

The launch page may present only capabilities visible in the current product: store, catalog, orders, customer records, shipping configuration/readiness, CRM, Sales Links, landing pages and reports. It must not present WhatsApp Live, Ads API, Public API or Custom Domains as live launch features. Future functionality should be omitted rather than disguised as available.

## Landing-page direction

1. Hero: category, “كل تجارتك من مكان واحد”, one 30-day CTA, and a real Matjari commerce visual.
2. Six-step operating flow: Store → Order → Shipping → Customer → CRM → Reports.
3. Capability grid grounded in implemented surfaces.
4. Three-step onboarding and a real dashboard screenshot.
5. Four launch cards with exact monthly prices and an explicit Free expiry statement.
6. Lifetime Access in a separate section.
7. FAQ that repeats the non-renewing 30-day Free policy and data-retention behavior.
8. Final CTA: “ابدأ شهرك المجاني”.
